# LandingSentinel — Sales Handoff (internal)

Internal document for the seller only. This is never shipped as a public page,
linked from the site, or included in the buyer package.

## After payment (runbook)

1. **Verify the Purchase record is paid.** Server-verified state only — never
   accept a buyer screenshot as proof of payment.
2. **Email the source package** to the purchase email on record.
3. **Attach/link the licence** — LICENSE.md and LICENSE-SUMMARY.md, exactly as
   they ship in the package.
4. **Send QUICKSTART.md** so the buyer starts on the verified fresh-install
   path.
5. **Offer installation support** — 30 days are included with the founding
   licence; make sure the buyer knows.
6. **Record fulfillment as delivered** in the Purchase record.

Keep every buyer interaction professional. Never make the buyer think the £349
software is incomplete without consulting — it is not. The licence and the
product stand on their own; engineering work is offered, never implied as
required.
